What does Shivara build?

Shivara develops websites, web applications, dashboards, AI agents and business automation workflows for startups and businesses.

Can Shivara develop websites for restaurants and cafés?

Yes. Shivara builds business websites, including websites for restaurants and cafés. Contact us to discuss the features you need.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that can assist with tasks such as responding to common questions or supporting business workflows. Its capabilities depend on the systems it connects to.

Can Shivara automate workflows?

Shivara works on lead automation, WhatsApp-related integrations and custom business tools. The right approach depends on your existing systems.
